## Summary

Manage Published Listings gives an Owner a single place to see every PG they publish on the platform and open the right one when they need to act. It matters because owners only keep inventory fresh when finding and updating a listing is quick and obvious, which supports accurate discovery for Users. Without this hub, scattered entry points make listing maintenance feel impractical and increase stale or neglected supply.

## User Story

As an Owner, I want to view and choose among my published listings in one place so that I can keep each property accurate and current without hunting through the app.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The owner can open a dedicated area in the owner-page module that is intended for managing their listings.
- The owner sees a list of their published PG listings, not other owners’ listings.
- Each row or card shows enough information for the owner to tell listings apart (for example, listing name or title plus at least one clear cue such as city or area).
- The owner can select a specific listing from the list and open that listing’s management view.
- When the owner has no published listings, the system shows an empty state that states there is nothing to manage yet.

## Priority

High — It is the practical center for the module’s must-have outcome that managing listings stays low-effort and sustainable over time.

## Dependencies

- `create-pg-listing`
