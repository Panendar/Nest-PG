## Objective

Help the Owner persona keep a PG visible, accurate, and inquiry-ready by moving listing management into a single, reliable platform workflow. This module exists so Owners can publish their PG, keep details and availability current, and present trustworthy information that leads to credible interest from Users.

## Success Outcomes

- Week-over-week growth in active owner listings managed through the platform.
- Higher share of owner listings that remain current and available for user discovery.
- Increased volume of credible user inquiries reaching Owners through active listings.
- Reduced marketplace trust issues caused by stale or incomplete owner information.
- [MISSING: Numeric targets and time-bound thresholds for the outcomes above are not defined in the source module documentation.]

## Must-Have Features

- Owners can create a PG listing so their property becomes discoverable on the platform.
- Owners can update listing details to keep property information accurate and useful for Users.
- Owners can add and maintain listing media so seekers can evaluate the PG with confidence.
- Owners can update availability status so Users are not misled by stale inventory.
- Owners can manage their published listing with low enough effort that keeping it current feels practical.

## Optional Features

- Owners can duplicate or reuse listing information to reduce effort when managing similar properties.
- Owners can receive prompts or reminders to review listings that may be becoming outdated.
- Owners can view simple signals about listing activity to understand whether their listing is generating interest.

## Out of Scope

- Anonymous visitor browsing, preview-search, and registration-conversion flows.
- User-side search, comparison, shortlist, and direct contact experiences.
- Admin moderation, governance, and marketplace-level operations.
- Broader owner business workflows unrelated to maintaining PG listing visibility and freshness.
